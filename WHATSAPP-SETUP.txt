VERDANT — WHATSAPP ORDER SETUP

1. Open script.js.
2. Find this line:
   const WHATSAPP_BUSINESS_NUMBER = '8801951848572';
3. Replace the placeholder with your WhatsApp Business number in international format,
   digits only. Bangladesh example: 8801XXXXXXXXX.
   Do not include +, spaces, brackets, or hyphens.
4. Save and upload the website files to your hosting.

HOW IT WORKS
- Customers add meals to the bag.
- They enter their name, phone number, delivery address, and preferred payment method.
- The website opens WhatsApp with the order summary pre-filled.
- The customer must press Send in WhatsApp to actually send the order.
- You confirm delivery fee, availability, and payment details directly with them.

PAYMENTS
This version does not collect card or mobile-wallet payments on the website.
Customers can choose cash on delivery, pay at pickup, or discuss payment on WhatsApp.
Never ask customers to enter card details into this website.

IMPORTANT
The WhatsApp number must be your business's WhatsApp number. The placeholder will not work
until you replace it. The website itself does not automatically receive or store orders;
the customer sends the prepared message from WhatsApp.
